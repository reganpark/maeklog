# Maeklog 02 logo kit

This folder contains the supplied 02 logo kit. The original SVG, PNG, preview and Korean notes are preserved unchanged; no geometry, colors or cropping were modified.

## Files

Use black assets on light backgrounds and white assets on dark backgrounds. SVGs contain outlined paths for both the symbol and wordmark, so displaying them does not require a font. All six PNGs have transparent backgrounds.

The [preview sheet](maeklog-02-preview.jpg) shows the kit on light and dark backgrounds. It has a background and is for reference; use the individual SVG or PNG assets in production.

## Use and notes

Keep the aspect ratio and built-in spacing. The supplied notes recommend clear space of at least approximately 15% of the symbol's width. Use the symbol alone at small sizes and check its narrow white gaps at the actual display size.

The [original Korean notes](README-KO.txt) preserve the design provenance, the Nimbus Sans Regular attribution for the outlined wordmark, and the stated limits of the rights review.

The earlier rejected concept is preserved in [archive/initial-concept](archive/initial-concept/README.md).
